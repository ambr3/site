Privacy

Plain version: I do not track you. Apps keep your stuff on your device unless you clearly opt into a named network call.

Updated 2026-11-16

This website

Seclusa is a static site. No accounts, no login, no ads, no search box that sends queries elsewhere.

I do not run analytics or third-party trackers. No third-party scripts, no remote fonts, no tracking cookies.

Theme is System, Light, or Dark. System follows the device and is not stored. Light or Dark lives in localStorage on this device only. A service worker may cache site files for offline use. Clear both from the browser anytime.

The site does not phone home on its own. Links you tap (Open, Source, GitHub, Matrix, email) go where they say. This site sends no Referer.

Hosting

The public site is on GitHub Pages. Like any host, GitHub may see request data (IP, user agent) to deliver the page. I do not get a personal analytics feed from that.

Seclusa apps

Apps are on the home store. In general:

  • Notes, Calendar, BP, Convert, Calc, and the games keep data in the browser. No account. Not designed to phone home with your notes, events, readings, scores, or calculator state.
  • Privacy check runs in your browser. Pasted policies, headers, and URLs stay local. Visit links leave only when you tap them.
  • Weather asks Open-Meteo for forecasts (no API key tied to you). Location is optional; you can search by place name instead.

Check each app’s README for the latest. Source is on GitHub.
