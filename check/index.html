Privacy check

App picks and local checks. Nothing leaves until you tap a link.

Early alpha — I am still adding apps as I go, which takes time. Not advice, just recommendations. Notes can be old or wrong. Nothing is fetched until you tap.

App recommendations

How I pick: I have personally used every app listed here. Personal favorites are marked, privacy comes before security when they trade off, and notes tie to a source link. Privacy is not the same as security. This is not advice.

If a note needs changing or correcting, email ambr3@dav3.mailer.me. This page does not send the message. Your mail app does, and only if you send it.

Privacy policy scan

Paste a policy. Local keyword heuristics flag risky clauses — not legal advice.

Email header check

Paste raw message headers. Parses auth results and common leak signals offline.

URL / HTTPS check

One URL per line. Local checks only — no remote fetch or certificate lookup.

About

Pasted policies, headers and URLs are not saved and never leave this device. These tools do not call the network. The Seclusa site may cache its own files so pages work offline. Pasted text is not part of that.

The Apps tab is stored in this page. A source link belongs to the sentence beside it. Corrections: ambr3@dav3.mailer.me.
