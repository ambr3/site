About

I make privacy-first PWAs so your data can stay on your device — no accounts, no tracking, no phoning home.

A PWA runs in the browser sandbox, needs HTTPS, and asks for permissions one at a time instead of taking a broad foothold on install.

They install like apps, work offline, and still let privacy browsers and normal site controls apply.

Everything here is open source so you can read the code yourself.
